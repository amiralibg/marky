What changes

  • One bar, not two. The Source/Live/Read switch and the ⋯ menu move into the title bar beside the tabs. The editor’s own 44px header goes away and the breadcrumb becomes a quiet eyebrow above the title.
  • The page is a sheet. The editor sits one tone lighter than the desk, with a 10px corner where it meets the sidebar. No borders around boxes, no cards.
  • Live and Read share a typeface, size and measure. Switching modes never reflows the page. Source alone goes monospace. Sans by default; Serif and Mixed are one click away in Appearance.
  • No scrollbars at rest. A thin thumb appears only while a pane is scrolling.
  • Mode is remembered. Whatever you picked last is what the next note, and the next launch, opens in.
  • The sidebar drops “Open”. Tabs already show open notes. That leaves Pinned and the tree, with search at the top and “New note” at the bottom.
  • A status line. Word count, reading time and save state sit in 11.5px at the foot of the sheet, replacing the floating “Saved” chip.
  • Read mode gets a progress hairline under the title bar in the accent color.

Three platforms, one layout

  • macOS: traffic lights sit inside the sidebar column, and the title bar is 44px with a full-height sidebar. Shortcuts render as ⌘.
  • Windows: 40px bar, 46px-wide caption buttons on the right, red close on hover, Segoe UI Variable for chrome. Shortcuts render as Ctrl.
  • Linux: 46px header bar with round GNOME-style buttons on the right. Works with or without client-side decorations.
  • Everything else is identical: same tokens, same spacing, same editor. Platform differences stay inside TitleBar.jsx.

Don’ts

  • No accent fills in chrome. The accent marks the caret, selection, links, the current heading and the active mode icon, and nothing else.
  • No shadows on anything that doesn’t float. Only menus, the palette and the selection bar get one.
  • No fourth radius. Use 6px for controls, 10px for panels and 999px for pills.
  • No toolbar row of formatting buttons. Formatting lives in the selection bar, the slash menu and shortcuts.
